SHABD SPY

Everyone gets the same word. One player gets something a little different. Give clues, then catch the spy.

Inspired by party-game classics Spyfall & Word Wolf.

How many players?
4
3 to 10 players
How to play

The phone gets passed to each player in turn. Tap & hold the box to see your word, let go and it hides again. Once everyone has seen their word, go around the group and each say one clue related to your word — without saying the word itself. Talk it over, then hit reveal to see who the spy was.
